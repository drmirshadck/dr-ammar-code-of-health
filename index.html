Frequently asked questions

1. How long is a consultation?

Each video consultation is 30 minutes, one to one with Dr Ammar.

2. Do I need to install an app?

No. The call runs in your phone or laptop browser on this website. Chrome and Safari both work.

3. How do I pay?

By UPI. Scan the QR code or tap to open your UPI app, then enter the transaction ID to continue to the calendar.

4. When do I get my room code?

After the clinic checks your payment, the room code is sent to the WhatsApp number you gave while booking.

5. Can I book for my child or a parent?

Yes. Enter the patient's name and age, and join the call together.

6. Is this for emergencies?

No. In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital or call 112.
